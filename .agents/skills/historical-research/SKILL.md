# Historical Research for Game Design
1. Separate documented historical claims from fictional story choices.
2. Prefer primary sources, archival references and corroborated scholarly material; provide sources and dates.
3. Mark disputed chronology, identities and exact locations as uncertain when evidence conflicts.
4. Convert historical references into practical art briefs: materials, architecture, clothing, era, and visual constraints.
5. Never present speculative dialogue or fictionalized actions by real people as verified history.
6. Track source and asset licensing before production use.
